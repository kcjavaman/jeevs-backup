jeevs-backup privacy policy

jeevs-backup is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission, so it can see only the files it created itself. It cannot read any other file in the Drive.

What it collects

Nothing. The backups are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload, and they stay in the owner's own Google Drive. No data is sent to anyone else, sold, shared, or used for any other purpose.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
